RESEARCH & EVIDENCE

We listened to understand the problem.

Our primary research helped us understand people's experiences with women's health information.

PRIMARY RESEARCH

What we learned

We conducted a small survey as part of our social entrepreneurship research.

12 survey responses
83% reported social/cultural difficulty
75% were somewhat confident finding reliable information
67% selected Dari as a preferred language

KEY FINDINGS

What participants told us

01

Social and cultural barriers matter

10 out of 12 respondents said social or cultural factors can make sensitive health discussions difficult.

10 / 12 respondents
02

People want understandable information

Simple and easy language was one of the most selected preferences for learning sensitive health topics.

5 / 12 respondents
03

Dari is an important language option

Dari was selected by many respondents as a preferred language for women's health information.

8 / 12 respondents

OPEN RESPONSES

Common themes

Reliable sources

Participants mentioned difficulty finding information they can trust.

Healthcare access

Some responses mentioned distance, cost, and limited healthcare professionals.

Fear of judgment

Some participants described social pressure and fear around discussing sensitive topics.

Language

Participants wanted information that is understandable and available in local languages.

FROM RESEARCH TO ACTION

The findings shaped our solution.

The research showed a need for reliable, simple, accessible, and culturally comfortable health information.
