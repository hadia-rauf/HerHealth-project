ABOUT THE PROJECT

Creating a safer way to learn about health.

HerHealth was created as a social entrepreneurship project focused on women's health awareness in Afghanistan.

THE PROBLEM

Information is not always easy to find.

Women and girls may have questions about their health but may not always have access to reliable information, healthcare professionals, or a comfortable environment for discussing sensitive topics.

Social and cultural barriers, cost, limited healthcare services, and a lack of understandable resources can make health education more difficult.

01

Limited awareness

Some women may not know where to find trustworthy health information.

02

Fear of judgment

Sensitive health topics can be difficult to discuss openly.

03

Access challenges

Professional healthcare and educational resources are not equally accessible to everyone.

OUR GOAL

Make reliable health education more accessible.

Our goal is to create a simple multilingual platform where women and girls can find understandable health information, trusted resources, and guidance about when professional healthcare may be needed.

OUR APPROACH

What HerHealth focuses on

01

Accessible

Information should be easy to find and understand.

02

Respectful

Sensitive health topics should be discussed without shame or judgment.

03

Practical

Information should help people understand possible next steps.

04

Evidence-informed

Educational content should be connected to trusted health information.

Important

HerHealth is an educational awareness project. The website does not diagnose medical conditions or replace a doctor, nurse, or other qualified healthcare professional.
